In the heart of the city,  
<br>
where the pavement pulsates,  
<br>
a hum of coffee machines,  
<br>
distant laughter layered thick—  
<br>
I wander through the corridors  
<br>
of absent conversations.
<br>

<br>
A pair of shoes, dusty by the door,  
<br>
whispers tales of days that frayed,  
<br>
threads unraveling like purpose,  
<br>
as I trace the outlines of your ghost  
<br>
in the corners of rooms  
<br>
too quiet for comfort.
<br>

<br>
Walls hold their breath,  
<br>
absorbing the secrets of late nights,  
<br>
when even the shadows found  
<br>
a reason to linger—  
<br>
I find comfort in their company,  
<br>
like an old song that forgets  
<br>
to fade into silence.
<br>

<br>
Time clatters in the kitchen,  
<br>
a clock ticks complaints of monotony,  
<br>
the clock hands lost in their routine,  
<br>
as I peel back layers of memory,  
<br>
the way an artist strips paint,  
<br>
revealing the chaos beneath.
<br>

<br>
It’s the longing for what never was,  
<br>
the half-formed dreams stacking up  
<br>
in an attic of fading light,  
<br>
while the world outside spins,  
<br>
in its bright, consuming cacophony,  
<br>
and I remain, charting the geography  
<br>
of solitude in these wrinkles of stillness.
<br>

<br>
I curl up with the quiet,  
<br>
finding solace in the corners,  
<br>
where dust settles on unspoken words  
<br>
meant to roll off lips,  
<br>
now only echo in baked thought,  
<br>
an unread letter  
<br>
tucked into the fabric of daydreams.
<br>

<br>
Tomorrow, perhaps, will arrive  
<br>
with its new noise,  
<br>
but tonight, I savor the silence,  
<br>
a lullaby wrapped warmly  
<br>
around my restless heart—  
<br>
in this soft chaos, I am whole,  
<br>
if only for a moment.
