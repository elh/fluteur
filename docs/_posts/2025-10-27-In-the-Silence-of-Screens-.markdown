In the silence of screens,  
<br>
pulses of light leap like whispers,  
<br>
connecting us to dreams woven in binary,  
<br>
a world where the real meets the imagined,  
<br>
where words flow like ink under pressure,  
<br>
dripping thoughts through pixelated veins.  
<br>

<br>
Faceless faces flicker,  
<br>
full of hope, despair, longing,  
<br>
each click a heartbeat,  
<br>
each scroll a sigh—  
<br>
we gather like moths at neon altars,  
<br>
searching for fire, for warmth,  
<br>
for someone who sees us,  
<br>
in the chaos of curated lives.  
<br>

<br>
Conversations heat the coldness of air,  
<br>
a symphony of silenced voices,  
<br>
muffled laughter spilled into silence,  
<br>
the sweet ache of tenderness untracked by time,  
<br>
tethered by the stretch of endless cables,  
<br>
while real moments slip through our fingers.  
<br>

<br>
We post pieces of our souls,  
<br>
carefully polished, carved to fit,  
<br>
but who are we in the stillness?  
<br>
Fingers poised above glass,  
<br>
we crave connection in echoes,  
<br>
never quite finding it in the mirror.  
<br>

<br>
Here, in the silence of screens,  
<br>
we become both infinite and alone,  
<br>
writing codes of longing,  
<br>
painting pixels of our intimacy,  
<br>
while shadows roam outside our walls,  
<br>
silent witnesses to the lives we live,  
<br>
not seen, not cyphered,  
<br>
but endlessly shared.
