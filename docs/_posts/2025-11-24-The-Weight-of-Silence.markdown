title:      The Weight of Silence

In the crowded room,  
<br>
voices weave a tapestry,  
<br>
threads of laughter,  
<br>
snippets of sorrow,  
<br>
but in the corner, I linger,  
<br>
embraced by a shroud of quiet,  
<br>
a cocoon spun with the ache   
<br>
of unspoken words.  
<br>

<br>
Eyes dart like playful moths,  
<br>
drawn to flames of familiarity,  
<br>
yet I, the uninvited moth,  
<br>
hover in shadows,   
<br>
grasping the heaviness  
<br>
of what could be said,  
<br>
but never spoken.  
<br>

<br>
Each breath rises,  
<br>
an insistent whisper,  
<br>
tapping at the door of my chest,  
<br>
fearful of the echo,  
<br>
the uncertainty of reaction,  
<br>
the weight, the gravity,  
<br>
of sentiments unshared.  
<br>

<br>
What if I were to speak?  
<br>
Would the air fracture,  
<br>
shift like tectonic plates  
<br>
underfoot?  
<br>
Or would they gather close,  
<br>
like explorers seeking treasure  
<br>
in the depths of my vulnerability?  
<br>

<br>
I sip my drink,  
<br>
the ice melting slowly,  
<br>
thoughts swirling like currents,  
<br>
the taste of unarticulated longing,  
<br>
the bittersweet tang of phantom connections,  
<br>
where every clink of glass  
<br>
is another moment  
<br>
slipping through unseen fingers.  
<br>

<br>
Away from the light,  
<br>
the cacophony softens,  
<br>
the world turns blurs,  
<br>
and I,  
<br>
a mere spectator,  
<br>
chart these unseen paths,  
<br>
these roads not taken,  
<br>
the quiet roads,  
<br>
where silence speaks louder than sound,  
<br>
and the pause between heartbeats  
<br>
can fill entire lifetimes.
