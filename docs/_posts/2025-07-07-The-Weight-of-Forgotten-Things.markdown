title:      The Weight of Forgotten Things
date:       2025-07-07 16:08:50

In the corners of a cluttered room,  
<br>
crumpled notes whisper secrets,  
<br>
the penmanship trembling,  
<br>
a faint echo of laughter,  
<br>
suspended in the weary air—  
<br>
ghosts of a conversation long slept.  
<br>

<br>
There’s a mug, half-full,  
<br>
coffee gone cold,  
<br>
it sits like a monument  
<br>
to a vibrant morning,  
<br>
now reduced to a fading aroma,  
<br>
the hope of connection,  
<br>
stored in porcelain and plaid.  
<br>

<br>
A faded photograph slides from under  
<br>
the stack of unread books,  
<br>
smiling faces frozen in time,  
<br>
but their names slip like water  
<br>
through trembling fingers.  
<br>
A single memory  
<br>
lost in a sea of blurred faces,  
<br>
turning to mist the moment it is grasped.  
<br>

<br>
And there’s the calendar,  
<br>
its days numbered and crossed out—  
<br>
each square a small surrender,  
<br>
a reminder of deadlines met,  
<br>
of promises twisted in the winds  
<br>
of distraction,  
<br>
or the quiet war of life against  
<br>
the ease of complacency.  
<br>

<br>
Between the walls, the spaces sigh,  
<br>
weary of keeping the secrets  
<br>
that could fill a thousand stories,  
<br>
it’s the weight of forgotten things,  
<br>
the crickets in the static,  
<br>
the silence that roars at midnight,   
<br>
that we carry  
<br>
when the world spins too fast,  
<br>
faster than the honoring  
<br>
what once was.  
<br>

<br>
I sift through the remnants,  
<br>
a curator of lost dreams,  
<br>
finding beauty in the mundane,  
<br>
understanding all the things we leave  
<br>
unspoken,  
<br>
the echoes we hold  
<br>
as we walk away,  
<br>
a brilliant mess spilling  
<br>
onto the floor,  
<br>
a dance of old hopes  
<br>
in new light,  
<br>
an embrace wrapped in memory,  
<br>
where past and present  
<br>
quietly meet.
