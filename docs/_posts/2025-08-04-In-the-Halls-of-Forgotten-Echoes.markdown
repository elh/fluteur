title:      In the Halls of Forgotten Echoes

Whispers travel through the corridors  
<br>
like errant thoughts floating above in a moonlit haze,  
<br>
unclaimed memories tangled in the fibers of time—  
<br>
laughter slipping between the cracks,  
<br>
a heartbeat caught in a shrug,  
<br>
the weathered sighs of a fading photograph.  
<br>

<br>
Once, in this very room,  
<br>
fingers danced upon the porcelain keys,  
<br>
the air thick with melodies,  
<br>
stories woven into harmonies,  
<br>
but now silence looms like a heavy curtain,  
<br>
the ghost of a past too stubborn to depart.  
<br>

<br>
The clock ticks—  
<br>
a relentless metronome,  
<br>
marking the seconds that slip away,  
<br>
each tick a pulse echoing outwards,  
<br>
searching for meaning in the mundane,  
<br>
as sunlight fractures like shattered glass  
<br>
across the splintered floorboards,  
<br>
painting abstract patterns of hopes unrealized.  
<br>

<br>
Concrete walls absorb the dreams,  
<br>
padlocks clasping secrets behind closed doors,  
<br>
echoes of ambition reverberate,  
<br>
in a dance as old as breath itself,  
<br>
craving release from the weight of expectation.  
<br>

<br>
Yet there’s a beauty in the disarray—  
<br>
in the way the dishes pile—  
<br>
and the coffee stains declare love  
<br>
to the mundane, the ordinary,  
<br>
the billowing drift of a thousand sighs  
<br>
that sit heavy in the air.  
<br>

<br>
Here, within these walls,  
<br>
we become our own architects,  
<br>
assembling joy from fragments of despair,  
<br>
shaping laughter from the brittle bones of silence—  
<br>
mosaic hearts beating,  
<br>
ringing out into the world  
<br>
with courage,  
<br>
truth,  
<br>
and the undeniable pulse of existence.
