Amidst the silent grove where shadows play,  
<br>
A whisper stirs the leaves in gentle dance,  
<br>
As twilight's breath wraps round the weary day,  
<br>
And secrets ride the breeze with soft advance.  
<br>

<br>
The oaks stand tall, their wisdom carved in bark,  
<br>
With gnarled limbs that weave the tales of time.  
<br>
They shelter moonlit paths where dreamers' hearts  
<br>
Can wander freely, lost in reverie.  
<br>

<br>
The roots descend to cradle earth's embrace,  
<br>
While branches cradle starlit skies above.  
<br>
Each rustling leaf sings ballads to the night,  
<br>
A symphony of nature's sweet refrain.  
<br>

<br>
O listen close, for in this hallowed space,  
<br>
The echoes of the ancients linger still.  
<br>
They speak of love, of loss, of dreams reborn,  
<br>
Of seasons turning with a patient grace.  
<br>

<br>
As morning breaks, the dawn will paint the sky  
<br>
With hues of gold, igniting life's anew;  
<br>
Yet in this sacred grove, the whispers lie,  
<br>
A timeless echo of the night's soft hue.  
<br>

<br>
So let us pause, beneath the watchful trees,  
<br>
And breathe the stories tucked in every grain;  
<br>
In stillness find the magic that still breathes,  
<br>
For in their strength, our fragile hearts remain.
