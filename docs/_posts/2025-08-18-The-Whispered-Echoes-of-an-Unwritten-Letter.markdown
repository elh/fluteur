title:      The Whispered Echoes of an Unwritten Letter

In the dim corner of a cluttered mind,  
<br>
thoughts slip and slide,  
<br>
like fish glimmering in murky water,  
<br>
each one a secret tethered to a name—  
<br>
an apology, a confession, a faded song.
<br>

<br>
I sit cloaked in shadows,  
<br>
poring over the unstated,  
<br>
where silence wraps its arms around me  
<br>
like an oversized sweater,  
<br>
a reassurance tinged with uncertainty.
<br>

<br>
Words stampede through the hallways of memory,  
<br>
each syllable heavy with unspoken weight,  
<br>
I gather them, too many,  
<br>
like mismatched socks tossed in a drawer,  
<br>
colorful remnants of lost conversations.
<br>

<br>
Do you sense the gravity of what remains unsaid?  
<br>
The laughter we once shared,  
<br>
now echoes smeared with distance,  
<br>
a ghostly duet of "What ifs" and "If onlys,"  
<br>
a dance that fades into the starlit void.
<br>

<br>
I wonder,  
<br>
in a world of pixelated exchanges,  
<br>
do you still reach for ink and paper,  
<br>
to carve your heart in cursive?  
<br>
Or has the art of breathing words  
<br>
been superseded by the sterile click of keys?
<br>

<br>
Your name, a fleeting comet,  
<br>
skims across my horizon,   
<br>
and I’m left tracing its fiery tail,  
<br>
afraid to launch my own  
<br>
and risk igniting that chasm between us.
<br>

<br>
So here I am, holding this unwritten letter,  
<br>
the weight of it sits in my throat,  
<br>
bulging with every breath I take,  
<br>
as if it were a key,  
<br>
but I can't find the door.
<br>

<br>
What is left to say when all that exists  
<br>
are the whispers of what could have been,  
<br>
thoughts that spiral like smoke rings,  
<br>
evaporating into an unseen sky,  
<br>
a language we once shared,  
<br>
now trapped,  
<br>
like echoes in a forgotten hallway.
