In the quiet of a flickering screen,  
<br>
where shadows dance upon walls,  
<br>
I sift through echoes of unsaid words,  
<br>
like petals caught in the wind—  
<br>
their fragrance faint yet haunting.
<br>

<br>
The clock ticks, a metronome of hesitation,  
<br>
as half-started thoughts linger,  
<br>
tangled in the spaces between our breaths,  
<br>
awash in a tide of what-ifs,  
<br>
where silence stretches like an unmade bed,  
<br>
and every sigh births a question,  
<br>
unanswered, profound,  
<br>
clinging to the air,  
<br>
thick with longing.
<br>

<br>
I know your laughter echoes somewhere,  
<br>
each note lingering like a promise unfulfilled,  
<br>
an unfinished symphony sitting heavy  
<br>
on the shelf of time—  
<br>
the dust settling on its shadows  
<br>
reminding me of consonants left unspoken.
<br>

<br>
And there, in the labyrinth of my mind,  
<br>
we arm wrestle with unshaped dreams,  
<br>
fissured hope racing through time’s embrace,  
<br>
as coffee cups grow cold,  
<br>
the steam escaping,  
<br>
an offering to the gods of missed connections.
<br>

<br>
So, we scribble our stories in neon lights,  
<br>
wide-eyed, daring the universe to listen,  
<br>
while the city outside yawns and shifts,  
<br>
and the hum of lives intertwine,  
<br>
creating an orchestra of solitude  
<br>
that plays just for us—  
<br>
yet we stand at the edge of our words,  
<br>
trembling, like children waiting for snow.
<br>

<br>
But perhaps in this quiet turmoil,  
<br>
we find grace,  
<br>
the beauty of the unvoiced,  
<br>
the art of holding someone in our heart,  
<br>
long after the last train has departed,  
<br>
cradling the echoes that slip through our fingers,  
<br>
sowing the seeds for a brighter tomorrow,  
<br>
one whispered truth at a time.
