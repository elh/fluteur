In the dim light of the coffee shop,  
<br>
where time seems to curve  
<br>
around broken dreams,  
<br>
we place our cups on the table,  
<br>
nervous steam mingling,  
<br>
a dance of silence before words.
<br>

<br>
You tell me about the job you didn't get,  
<br>
the rejection that shattered your confidence,  
<br>
as if confidence were glass,  
<br>
fragile yet weighty in the palm.  
<br>
I nod, not knowing how to weave comfort  
<br>
out of the threads of my own unspooled fears.
<br>

<br>
The clock ticks louder than laughter,  
<br>
a metronome to our small talk,  
<br>
each tick an uninvited guest,  
<br>
pressing against the musty air,  
<br>
reminding us, this is not a movie,  
<br>
there isn’t a happy ending—  
<br>
just the mundane truth of tomorrow.
<br>

<br>
Your stories spill, one by one,  
<br>
like the last drops of coffee,  
<br>
bitter and cold,  
<br>
but beautiful in their honesty.  
<br>
Friends have melted into acquaintances,  
<br>
like ice cubes in sun—  
<br>
I search your eyes for the warmth  
<br>
that once sparked joy  
<br>
but it flickers like the candle at the counter,  
<br>
dancing dangerously close to extinguishing.
<br>

<br>
What do we hold onto  
<br>
when the familiar fades?  
<br>
These fleeting moments  
<br>
stretched across the table,  
<br>
the silence filling the spaces  
<br>
where laughter once echoed,  
<br>
our dreams clustering in corners,  
<br>
deflated balloons.
<br>

<br>
I wish for clarity,  
<br>
for words that shape the fog,  
<br>
for the next chapter to leap off the page—  
<br>
but instead, here, we sit,  
<br>
a still frame caught in time,  
<br>
the world bustling beyond these walls,  
<br>
unaware of the gravity of our small moment.
<br>

<br>
Outside, the rain blurs the skyline,  
<br>
a veil over a restless city,  
<br>
while inside, we find closure in confessions,  
<br>
in unmasking the selves we hide,  
<br>
echoing to the end, softly,  
<br>
the unspoken promise of what we were,  
<br>
and what we can never be.  
<br>

<br>
Let’s clink our mugs one last time,  
<br>
to the space between heartbeats,  
<br>
to the gentle, aching goodbye,  
<br>
to a chapter written in the warmth  
<br>
of half-finished thoughts,  
<br>
in a café where memories linger  
<br>
like the scent of roasted beans.
