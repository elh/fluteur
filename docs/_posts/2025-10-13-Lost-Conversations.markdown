The phone rings in the dim light of the kitchen, a ghost of what once was; the voice that lingers in your mind, like an old song with scratched records, is an echo you replay, each note a vulnerability laid bare. Text messages pop up like fireflies in the dark, ephemeral sparks of connection fluttering in a world saturated with static, where silence is a fickle companion. You find yourself scrolling through the archives of what was never said, the “I should have” and “what ifs” fluttering like moths drawn to a flickering bulb. 
<br>

<br>
The coffee pot gurgles a reminder that mornings come quick, painting the walls with hope, yet each sip tastes like memories steeped too long. In the reflection of the window, your thoughts become architectural, building castles of conversations not had, bridges over bottled up emotions—all constructed on pages drenched with ink, where every "maybe" feels like an unturned stone in a riverbed of doubt.
<br>

<br>
And in the stillness that follows, you gather your words like autumn leaves, vibrant and flaking, preparing for a future that feels like it holds the promise of thaw. You crouch down at the edge of possibility, yet the air hangs thick with what’s left unsaid, whispering secrets that curl around your shoulders like a shawl woven from the fabric of hesitation. 
<br>

<br>
Tomorrow, you think, tomorrow you’ll reclaim the channel, pop the cork on pent-up ideas like champagne bubbles rushing toward the surface. Ghostly echoes linger in the corners of your heart—a pulse, a call, a question suspended in time, reminding you that each lost conversation is merely the prelude to a new beginning.
