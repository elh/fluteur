In the stillness of the morning  
<br>
the kettle hums a lullaby,  
<br>
each drip a droplet of intention,  
<br>
my fingers cradle a porcelain fortress,  
<br>
the steam whispers secrets,  
<br>
a muted world wakes,  
<br>
the scent of roasted dreams rising,  
<br>
my thoughts,  
<br>
like sugar, swirl, dissolve.  
<br>

<br>
Outside, jagged skyscrapers pierce the sky,  
<br>
a chorus of honks,  
<br>
the city breathes  
<br>
as I sit,  
<br>
nestled in the cocoon of my corner booth,  
<br>
where strangers become ghosts,  
<br>
passing shadows beneath dim light.  
<br>

<br>
Words drift through the café,  
<br>
clinking cups,  
<br>
shuffling papers,  
<br>
a symphony of lives colliding,  
<br>
disparate and familiar,  
<br>
each face a story  
<br>
half-told,  
<br>
echoing fragments of laughter and sorrow.  
<br>

<br>
Time bends like a spoon,  
<br>
nervous fingers tap out rhythms  
<br>
on mottled vinyl tablecloths,  
<br>
the barista’s smile—a currency,  
<br>
exchanged for warmth,  
<br>
as the world outside spins  
<br>
in an endless clockwork dance.  
<br>

<br>
I toss coins of thought,  
<br>
memories bubbling over  
<br>
like too much cream in my cup,  
<br>
the sweetness of it lingering,  
<br>
bitter edges softly soothed,  
<br>
in this sacred ritual  
<br>
where words hang thick in the air,  
<br>
and the ordinary feels divine.
