title:      The Traces We Leave Behind

In the quiet corners of crowded rooms,  
<br>
voices blend, a tapestry woven  
<br>
of laughter and sighs,  
<br>
the minty breath of someone’s last joke  
<br>
clinging to the air like old perfume.
<br>

<br>
We pass like ships in the night,  
<br>
our stories mingling momentarily,  
<br>
stardust flickers in the light’s fragile glow,  
<br>
each encounter a spark, an ember,  
<br>
cooling as it slips away into memory.
<br>

<br>
The clicking keys of forgotten dreams,  
<br>
the heavy thud of a heart carrying secrets,  
<br>
we etch our names on ephemeral surfaces,  
<br>
a fleeting graffiti of existence,  
<br>
traced in the dust of a crowded table.
<br>

<br>
Listen to the echo of a silent call,  
<br>
the space between words stretching tight,  
<br>
the anticipation of a bus that never arrives,  
<br>
that phone call you never make,  
<br>
the moment stretching infinitely before you.
<br>

<br>
Yet still we seek connection,  
<br>
navigating the labyrinth of human hearts,  
<br>
grappling with the immense fragility,  
<br>
of love that flickers, burns, then fades,  
<br>
a ghostly handprint on a fogged-up window.
<br>

<br>
So here we are,  
<br>
the architects of our own absence,  
<br>
each step a testament,  
<br>
to the joy, the pain, the indifference,  
<br>
the rattling dice of chance, always rolling. 
<br>

<br>
What do we leave when we move on?  
<br>
A faint trace of laughter in a hallway,  
<br>
a half-finished song that lingers in the air,  
<br>
and the weight of moments that once felt  
<br>
like a universe contained in a single glance.
