In the stillness of twilight,  
<br>
where shadows weave tales into the fabric of night,  
<br>
I hear the whispers of the forgotten,  
<br>
echoing through the rustling leaves  
<br>
as if they carry secrets from the depths of a lost world.  
<br>

<br>
They speak in a language known only to the stars,  
<br>
a dialect of hope etched on the skin of the moon,  
<br>
each word a fleeting ember,  
<br>
tangible and yet ephemeral,  
<br>
like the sigh of a breeze that shivers  
<br>
through the crevices of time.  
<br>

<br>
Here, in this twilight sanctuary,  
<br>
I gather their voices,  
<br>
collecting them like fallen petals,  
<br>
a mosaic of dreams long buried  
<br>
beneath the weight of careless mornings.  
<br>

<br>
Time, that elusive specter,  
<br>
drips like honey from the eaves of memory,  
<br>
blending laughter with sorrow,  
<br>
and in this alchemy, I find myself—  
<br>
a wanderer suspended between what was  
<br>
and what may never be.  
<br>

<br>
I breathe in the night’s embrace,  
<br>
finding solace in the constellations  
<br>
that align, destined to guide me  
<br>
through the labyrinth of my thoughts,  
<br>
where every twist and turn reveals  
<br>
untamed landscapes of the heart.  
<br>

<br>
So let the forgotten dance,  
<br>
let their whispers twine around my spirit,  
<br>
for in the tapestry of existence,  
<br>
they weave a hymn everlasting,  
<br>
a testament to those who dared to dream  
<br>
in the shadows of the unsung.
