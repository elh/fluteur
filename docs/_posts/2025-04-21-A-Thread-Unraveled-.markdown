In the café corner,  
<br>
a porcelain cup of silence,  
<br>
steam swirling like secrets  
<br>
in the amber light,  
<br>
as laughter drips,  
<br>
a gentle cascade of memories  
<br>
we thought we’d lost.  
<br>

<br>
Fingers tap a rhythmic defiance  
<br>
on worn wooden tables,  
<br>
the click of heels on polished floors—  
<br>
a metronome of expectation,  
<br>
each face a collage,  
<br>
each gaze a canvas,  
<br>
painted with longing,  
<br>
desire’s brush strokes deliberate and bold.  
<br>

<br>
Conversations ricochet like marbles,  
<br>
each one a spark,  
<br>
igniting thoughts,  
<br>
arcing across the smoky air,  
<br>
a tapestry of stories woven  
<br>
from the fabric of strangers who know  
<br>
the softness of unspoken dreams.  
<br>

<br>
I watch a woman’s eyes gleam,  
<br>
an aviary trapped in glass,  
<br>
daydreams fluttering against  
<br>
the windowpane of reality,  
<br>
while a man in the corner  
<br>
pulls at his collar,  
<br>
as if choking on unfit truths,  
<br>
the weight of them sinking,  
<br>
like stones thrown into a deep abyss.  
<br>

<br>
And somewhere, an old song  
<br>
crackles through the speakers,  
<br>
nostalgia clings like a comfortable sweater,  
<br>
the notes weaving an invisible thread,  
<br>
binding us in moments  
<br>
that balance on the edge of tomorrow—  
<br>
the transient and the eternal  
<br>
waltzing in tandem.  
<br>

<br>
We exist in between—  
<br>
between the spoken and the silent,  
<br>
the joyous and the melancholy,  
<br>
the glass-half-full perspective,  
<br>
and the bitter dregs of resignation.  
<br>
What are we but the sum of these intermissions,  
<br>
the clinking of mugs, the sigh of surrender,  
<br>
the rush of connections  
<br>
that ebb and flow like tides  
<br>
against the shore of our being?  
<br>

<br>
In this cozy haunt,  
<br>
bodies entwined yet isolated,  
<br>
the world spins beyond these walls,  
<br>
but here, in this moment,  
<br>
we are all fragments of a larger whole,  
<br>
the café continues to pulse,  
<br>
an eternal heartbeat  
<br>
echoing in the quiet corners of life.
