In the quiet hours when twilight spills,  
<br>
the world mellows into sighs,  
<br>
and shadows stretch like lazy cats across the pavement—  
<br>
I wander, a collector of moments,  
<br>
cupping fragments of laughter and grief  
<br>
in the palms of my hands.
<br>

<br>
Here, beneath a sky bruised with hues of lavender,  
<br>
trees dance their ancient dance,  
<br>
twisting limbs whispering secrets  
<br>
to the cool fingers of evening—  
<br>
and I can almost hear the chorus of stars  
<br>
knitting patches of starlight into the fabric of night.
<br>

<br>
Footprints of strangers linger on the cobbled path,  
<br>
echoes of dreams chased down alleyways,  
<br>
fragments of songs left unsung,  
<br>
as the moon blinks awake, shy and distant,  
<br>
and the world exhales, soft and slow  
<br>
beneath the weight of the cosmos.
<br>

<br>
Time, that slippery sprite,  
<br>
meanders through my thoughts,  
<br>
pulling me into the tapestry of what was,  
<br>
each thread vibrant with the stories of loss and love,  
<br>
caressing the weary heart of an insatiable wanderer.
<br>

<br>
Oh, let me be a vessel for the fleeting,  
<br>
a vessel for the whispers of the ephemeral,  
<br>
as laughter spills from a child's lips,  
<br>
or a lover's farewell drips heavy into the dusk—  
<br>
for in these fragile cadences,  
<br>
we are woven into the breath of the universe,  
<br>
where every heartbeat is a reminder  
<br>
of the beauty found in the impermanence of being.
