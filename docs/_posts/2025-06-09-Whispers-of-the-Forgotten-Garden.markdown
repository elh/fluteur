In the dappled sunlight, where shadows stretch and sigh,  
<br>
A garden once brimming, now whispers of lost dreams.  
<br>
Petals drift like memories, unfurling softly,  
<br>
Beneath the heavy breath of the ancient oak.  
<br>

<br>
Here, the roses bled crimson stories,  
<br>
Of lovers’ laughter and sweet morning dew,  
<br>
While lilacs curled their fragrance  
<br>
Around the secrets of the sun.  
<br>

<br>
Time has threaded through the leaves,  
<br>
Twisting tales into the gnarled branches—  
<br>
An epilogue written in the language of weeds,  
<br>
As ivy chokes the whispers of the past.  
<br>

<br>
Yet still, a lone sunflower turns its radiant head,  
<br>
Defiant against the shadows,  
<br>
Embracing each breath of wind,  
<br>
In the relentless pursuit of light.  
<br>

<br>
And somewhere, beneath the softening earth,  
<br>
A buried seed dreams of tomorrow,  
<br>
Yearning to break through this slumber,  
<br>
To reach for the sky with unguarded hope.  
<br>

<br>
Even amidst decay, life remembers—  
<br>
The heartbeat of the seasons,  
<br>
In a forgotten garden of endless possibility,  
<br>
Where all things may bloom anew,  
<br>
When given a chance to rise again.
