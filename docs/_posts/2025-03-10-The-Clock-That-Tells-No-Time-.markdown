title:      The Clock That Tells No Time

The clock in the corner,  
<br>
its hands frozen in forgotten moments,  
<br>
a relic of promises once whispered  
<br>
over steaming coffee cups,  
<br>
now a ghost in its own frame,  
<br>
mocking the rhythms of life  
<br>
with silence dripping like honey.  
<br>

<br>
Walls painted with yesterday's laughter,  
<br>
their echoes hang heavy in the air,  
<br>
each brushstroke  
<br>
a memory caged in gloss,  
<br>
while the sun, uninvited,  
<br>
peeks through the dusty blinds,  
<br>
casting stripes of light  
<br>
on the carpet where dreams lay to rest.  
<br>

<br>
We dance in the shadows,  
<br>
shuffling through the ballroom of our minds,  
<br>
everything feels louder in quiet rooms,  
<br>
the rustle of fading ambitions,  
<br>
the shattering of glass dreams,  
<br>
and in the noise of our stillness,  
<br>
we find the spark to ignite the mundane,  
<br>
breathing rhythm back into absence.  
<br>

<br>
Whispers of the heart beat softly,  
<br>
keeping time on a metronome of longing,  
<br>
as we navigate the maze of existence,  
<br>
chasing moments as they scatter,  
<br>
like marbles kicked hastily underfoot,  
<br>
while we try to gather them,  
<br>
cradling fractured colors in our palms,  
<br>
and asking the universe to make sense  
<br>
of this cacophony of silence.  
<br>

<br>
In the stillness, we exist,  
<br>
the clock telling us nothing and everything,  
<br>
reminding us that time is a loop,  
<br>
a circle of return, and we,  
<br>
we are the makers of rhythm,  
<br>
the keepers of our own tempo,  
<br>
even as the clock stands witness  
<br>
to the relentless march of all that fades.
