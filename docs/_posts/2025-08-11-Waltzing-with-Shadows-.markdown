title:      Waltzing with Shadows

In the quiet of dawn,  
<br>
light spills across the horizon,  
<br>
unfurling like ribbons in the wind,  
<br>
stitching dreams to the fabric of the day,  
<br>
as shadows waltz with truth,  
<br>
whispering secrets of the night.
<br>

<br>
The trees stand sentry,  
<br>
their branches knit in a murmured lullaby,  
<br>
each leaf a page of a forgotten story,  
<br>
crisp with age,  
<br>
rustling softly under the breath of time—  
<br>
a dialogue between the earth and sky.
<br>

<br>
I walk the cobblestone street,  
<br>
feel its pulse beneath my feet,  
<br>
each stone a memory,  
<br>
each crack a sigh,  
<br>
echoes of laughter from unseen ghosts,  
<br>
as the sun kisses the rooftops,  
<br>
reshaping the familiar into the extraordinary.
<br>

<br>
Here, the mundane transcends,  
<br>
a cup of coffee steams conversation,  
<br>
laughter erupts like wildflowers,  
<br>
and in the chaos of streetcars and sirens,  
<br>
I find solace—  
<br>
a vibrant tapestry woven with strands of connection.
<br>

<br>
I pause,  
<br>
to cradle the moment,  
<br>
breathing in the scent of possibility,  
<br>
as the world spins on,  
<br>
waltzing with shadows,  
<br>
forever in pursuit of light.  
<br>

<br>
And at dusk, when the sky blazes,  
<br>
a kaleidoscope of change,  
<br>
I will surrender to the twilight,  
<br>
trusting the silhouette of what I am becoming,  
<br>
waiting, for the night to share  
<br>
its quiet truths,  
<br>
and the whispered promises of dawn.
