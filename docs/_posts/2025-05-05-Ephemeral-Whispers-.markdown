In the quiet hour before dawn,  
<br>
when the world is wrapped in shadows,  
<br>
I hear the soft sigh of wishes,  
<br>
slipping through the cracks of slumber,  
<br>
as if yesterday's dreams,  
<br>
like puffs of smoke,  
<br>
still linger in the cool air.  
<br>

<br>
The moon hangs like a silver coin,  
<br>
a forgotten promise,  
<br>
while stars, like scattered thoughts,  
<br>
twinkle their secrets,  
<br>
unraveling the tapestry of night,  
<br>
woven with threads of solitude and hope.  
<br>

<br>
I wander the spaces between moments,  
<br>
where echoes of laughter refuse to fade,  
<br>
caught in the fragile web of memory,  
<br>
each heartbeat a reminder  
<br>
that life is an ever-turning page,  
<br>
inked with joys and sorrows alike.  
<br>

<br>
There in the breeze,  
<br>
whispers of forgotten times,  
<br>
and the scent of blooming lilacs,  
<br>
awaken the ghosts of childhood,  
<br>
who dance around me,  
<br>
in the flicker of fireflies,  
<br>
their light a gentle rebellion.  
<br>

<br>
Time flows, an untamed river,  
<br>
carrying me toward horizons unknown,  
<br>
as I gather the petals of today,  
<br>
pressing them between the lines of my heart,  
<br>
for every fleeting moment,  
<br>
is a story waiting to be told,  
<br>
an ephemeral treasure,  
<br>
slipping like sand through fingers.  
<br>

<br>
And so, as the sun begins to rise,  
<br>
painting the sky in strokes of gold,  
<br>
I stand,  
<br>
a quiet witness  
<br>
to the beauty of the ordinary,  
<br>
to the delicate balance of chaos and grace,  
<br>
in this ever-evolving dance of existence.
