In the heart of an abandoned park,  
<br>
where laughter curls into memory,  
<br>
rusty swings sway gently,  
<br>
their chains a melody of ghosts,  
<br>
and the grass, once manicured,  
<br>
now dances wild under the sun's soft gaze.
<br>

<br>
Beneath the shadows of towering trees,  
<br>
stories linger like fog on a misty morning,  
<br>
whispers of children running,  
<br>
barefoot, through puddles of light,  
<br>
their joy carved into the air  
<br>
like a promise from the past.
<br>

<br>
A bench, weathered and worn,  
<br>
cradles the weight of lost conversations,  
<br>
where lovers once etched initials—  
<br>
time's hand now softens their resolve,  
<br>
and the world spins on, unremembered,  
<br>
each breeze a sigh from the forgotten.
<br>

<br>
The old fountain, dry as a secret,  
<br>
stands sentinel to the dreams poured out,  
<br>
where coins once twinkled like stars,  
<br>
impossible wishes made by careless hands,  
<br>
now lies dormant, waiting for rain,  
<br>
or a child's laugh to break the silence.
<br>

<br>
Here, the echoes weave through the branches,  
<br>
inviting the curious to listen,  
<br>
to lean in, to feel the pulse of what was,  
<br>
as dusk drapes a velvet quilt over the scene,  
<br>
and the fireflies summon the dusk’s magic,  
<br>
an invitation to remember, to reclaim,  
<br>
the beauty of what may never return.
