In the quiet of dawn,  
<br>
where shadows stretch like dreams,  
<br>
the air unfurls with the scent of wild thyme,  
<br>
and the sun spills golden secrets  
<br>
between blades of grass.
<br>

<br>
Each dewdrop captures the whispers  
<br>
of a thousand yesterdays,  
<br>
while reluctant petals unfurl their faces,  
<br>
softly brushing against the chill,  
<br>
as if recounting tales to the winds.
<br>

<br>
There, amidst the wildflowers,  
<br>
a butterfly flits,  
<br>
its wings painted with sunlight,  
<br>
as if the artist had seen  
<br>
the laughter of the clouds.
<br>

<br>
The old oak stands sentinel,  
<br>
its gnarled branches embracing the sky,  
<br>
a witness to both laughter and tears,  
<br>
where children carved their names,  
<br>
and lovers etched promises on its bark.
<br>

<br>
Beneath the canopy of secrets,  
<br>
time slips like water through fingers,  
<br>
and the rhythm of a heartbeat  
<br>
calls forth the pulse of the earth,  
<br>
in harmony with the flutter of wings.
<br>

<br>
Oh, how the meadow holds its breath,  
<br>
in the moment before twilight descends,  
<br>
where colors bleed like emotions,  
<br>
and the horizon blushes with the echo  
<br>
of a day well-loved.
<br>

<br>
Here, in this sacred silence,  
<br>
we become part of the whispers,  
<br>
the boundless stories woven into the wind,  
<br>
lost and found,  
<br>
like scattered seeds,  
<br>
waiting to bloom once more.
