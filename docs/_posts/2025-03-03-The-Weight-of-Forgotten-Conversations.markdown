title:      The Weight of Forgotten Conversations
date:       2025-03-03 16:08:06

In the quiet hum of the city,  
<br>
voices swirl, a tapestry of fragments,  
<br>
each syllable a weightless feather,  
<br>
yet they pile up like autumn leaves,  
<br>
crisp and dry, forgotten.  
<br>

<br>
Fingers tap like raindrops on glass,  
<br>
electric pulses split the air—  
<br>
I wonder who listens,  
<br>
who holds these stolen breaths,  
<br>
the echoes of a laugh,  
<br>
the tremor in a whispered secret,  
<br>
laughter fading into the night.  
<br>

<br>
I wander through the ghost streets,  
<br>
past neon halos and mottled shadows,  
<br>
where confessions seep  
<br>
from cracked sidewalks,  
<br>
a mosaic of longing,  
<br>
stories trapped in the velvet gloom.  
<br>

<br>
In cafés teeming with strangers,  
<br>
we sip our warmed-up solitude,  
<br>
glance over our shoulders,  
<br>
as if the past might run up  
<br>
and embrace us unbidden,  
<br>
like a dream we cannot recall  
<br>
but feel etched in our bones.  
<br>

<br>
Oh, the weight of our silence,  
<br>
like the night pressing down,  
<br>
until every word feels too raw,  
<br>
the aching need to speak,  
<br>
to share the mundane, the chaotic,  
<br>
to unravel and weave the threads  
<br>
into a new dawn,  
<br>
but we clutch our secrets,  
<br>
afraid they might abate.  
<br>

<br>
Yet in this frenetic cosmic dance,  
<br>
where hearts strive to find rhythm,  
<br>
the unspoken lingers,  
<br>
giving voice to the pulse of humanity,  
<br>
buried beneath layers of “what if”  
<br>
and the comfort of familiar scars,  
<br>
our stories breathe,  
<br>
unfurling like old photographs,  
<br>
cradled in the arms of time—  
<br>
forgotten, yet never lost.
